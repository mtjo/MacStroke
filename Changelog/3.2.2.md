## MacStroke 3.2.2

**新增 / Added**

- A gesture can now hit the window under your cursor instead of whatever happened to be frontmost when you let go (issue #22). The report was exactly this: cursor parked on the browser, gesture drawn there, ⌘ shortcut lands in the IDE. With the new switch on, the app that owns the window under your **release point** is the app the rule is matched against **and** the app the keys are sent to.

  新增「手势打到光标下的窗口」（issue #22）。用户报的就是那句：光标停在浏览器上画手势，快捷键却打进 IDE。开关一开，松手那个点压在谁家的窗上，规则就按那家挑、注入的键也投给那家 —— 画轨迹的那扇窗和挨那一下键的那扇窗从此是同一扇。

- Off by default, and off is the old behaviour down to the last microsecond: while the switch is off the on-screen window list is never queried at all. The condition is a property in the module, not a branch sprinkled at the call site, precisely so that "off but still scanning windows" cannot quietly come back in a later edit.

  默认关，而关掉是关到根上：开关关着时**压根不去抓那张在屏窗口表**，一次都不抓。这个条件做成模块里的一个属性、不是散在调用点的 if —— 因为「关着却照样每抬手抓一遍窗口」这种回退在行为和界面上都看不出来，只有钉在那一处才咬得住。手势识别的耗时与抬手手感不变。

- The second switch decides what happens after the action lands. Leave it on and the focus goes back to the app you released over about 0.6 s later, so a gesture is a one-shot delivery. Turn it off and one gesture becomes the same as one click on that window: focus stays there and everything you type next goes into it.

  第二颗开关管「投完还不还焦点」。开着 = 过约 0.6 秒把焦点还给抬手那一刻那一家，一次手势只是「顺手送一发键」；关掉 = 画一次手势等于点了一下那扇窗，焦点留在那家，接下来打的字全进那里。

- Three kinds of action never take part, because they were never about who is frontmost: clicks at a coordinate, copy-to-clipboard, and the screenshot overlay. The switch is greyed, not hidden, for the rest; the other controls on the page are unchanged.

  三类动作不参与：按坐标点击、复制文本、弹出截图 —— 它们跟谁是前台无关。截图那档尤其不能还焦：0.6 秒后那一手会把刚弹出来的遮罩抢走。界面沿用这一条线的口径，不参与的行只灰不藏。

- Judging "did the focus actually move" does not ask the usual helpers. `NSWorkspace.frontmostApplication` was measured to still report the old app in the very same moment, and `NSRunningApplication.isActive` is a cache fed by async notifications — and this code runs inside the gesture's own event-tap callback, where the runloop doesn't turn until the callback returns. Success is read from two things that are true synchronously: the target app's own `AXFrontmost`, and the owner of the topmost ordinary window in the window-server list.

  「焦点到底跳过去没有」不信常规那几个查询：实测在同一瞬间 `NSWorkspace.frontmostApplication` 还报旧家，`NSRunningApplication.isActive` 是异步通知喂的缓存，而这一段跑在手势自己的事件回调里 —— 回调没返回 runloop 就不转，缓存可以整段预算期都是旧的。判据只取当场为真的两条：目标 App 自报的 `AXFrontmost`，加上窗口服务里层 0 最前那扇普通窗的主人。

**注意 / Notes**

- When the jump fails, the action is not dropped. It is posted exactly as before, to the app that was already frontmost, and the log says so (which route was tried, what the accessibility write returned, who received the keystroke). Silently losing a gesture you clearly drew is harder to debug than a gesture that landed in the wrong window.

  跳不过去时**不吞掉动作**：照样按老行为投给当时的前台，日志写清「没跳过去（raise=? / 写 AXFrontmost=?）、这一发投给了谁」。静默丢掉一条画完的手势，比跳错家更难查。

- The focus jump is visible: the other window comes forward for a moment. That is inherent to the approach taken here — the alternative (posting straight to a process id) measured 1315 ms in Chrome and 732 ms in Mail against this route's 222 ms / 491 ms, and its behaviour differs per app because text still needs a key window to land in a field.

  跳过去那一下看得见（对方窗口会闪一下），这是所选方案的固有代价。另一条路（绕过前台、直投进程号）本机实测 Chrome 1315ms、邮件 732ms，比这一条慢一截，而且逐家行为不一致 —— 文本要落进输入框还得靠 key window。

- Restoring the focus yields to you: if by the time the 0.6 s is up the frontmost app is no longer the one we jumped to, you moved on your own and nothing is taken back. The number is measured headroom (a posted key takes 0.2–0.5 s to become a visible action), not a taste setting. Taking the focus back any earlier would snatch the keystroke we just sent.

  还焦会给你让路：到点时前台已经不是我们跳过去那一家，说明你自己切走了，这一手不抢。0.6 秒这个数是实测 0.2～0.5 秒往上留的余量，不是手感偏好 —— 提前抢回来等于把刚投出去那一发也抢回自己家。这一段不 sleep，走延迟队列，你不会为它多等。

- If the release point sits on our own canvas, the bubble, or the preferences window, the stroke is attributed to the app underneath, not to MacStroke. Black- and white-listed apps are honoured at the judgement stage: a cursor resting on an app you excluded means that app never receives an injected key, even though the rule that matched belongs to a different app.

  松手点压在自家的画布、划词气泡或偏好窗上时，不归给 MacStroke，往下找第一个够格的那家。黑白名单在判定阶段就问：黑名单的语义是「别在这家里动手」，光标压在黑名单那家、前台是另一家时，不会拿别家的规则往你明确排除的家里投键。

- Everything the decision did (or refused to do) is in the log under `[StrokeTarget]`, one line per reason: feature off, no ordinary window at that point, only our own windows, that app isn't an activatable regular app, no bundle id, filtered by the list, same app as the front one, attributed. Eight reasons, but "feature off" deliberately writes nothing, so the useful lines don't get buried. Multi-screen users are worth a second look after upgrading: the release point is flipped into window-server coordinates in exactly one place, and a mistake there shows up only when you draw on the second display.

  判定的每一步都在日志里（限定进程、带 `--info --debug` 再看，别漏了 Df 那档），口径照代码里的原话：`功能没开`／`松手点没有普通层窗口`／`松手点上只有我们自己的窗`／`那家不是可激活的普通 App`／`那家没有 bundleId，规则没法按它匹配`／`那家被黑白名单过滤掉了，按老行为投前台`／`光标下本来就是前台那家，无需跳转`／`归给光标下的窗口`，共八档 —— 其中「功能没开」那档特意不写行，免得把有用的埋掉，所以日志里最多见到七档。接了副屏的值得多试一次：抬手点的坐标翻转只有一处，翻错了只在副屏上露馅（主屏上下各画一次、副屏上下各画一次就能分辨）。

- Two new keys, `strokeTargetsCursorWindow` (default off) and `strokeTargetRestoresFocus` (default on); the latter only does anything while the first is on. Nothing else on any settings page moved, no new permission is requested, and the two timing constants are constants — not sliders.

  新增两把偏好键：`strokeTargetsCursorWindow` 默认关、`strokeTargetRestoresFocus` 默认开（后者只在前者开着时有意义）。别的页面一行没动，不多要任何权限；两个时间常数是常量，不铺滑杆。
