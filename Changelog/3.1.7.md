## MacStroke 3.1.7

**新增 / Added**

- A new preferences tab, "Proximity Lock", turns a Bluetooth LE wearable into a key for this Mac: walk away and the screen locks, come back and it can unlock itself

  偏好设置多了一页「靠近锁屏」：戴的蓝牙设备就是这台 Mac 的钥匙 —— 人走开屏幕锁上，人回来可以自动解开

- Presence is decided from signal strength with two thresholds instead of one: the average has to climb above the "back" threshold to count as present and fall below the "away" threshold to count as gone, so a few decibels of jitter near the door no longer locks and unlocks in a row. The gap between the two thresholds, how many seconds to keep counting after you leave, and how long without a single advertisement before the device is treated as lost are all adjustable

  在不在旁边由信号强度按两个阈值判定：滑窗均值要高过「回来」阈值才算在场，低过「走开」阈值才算离场，两个阈值之间留了滞回 —— 门口那几 dB 的抖动不再来回锁解。两个阈值差多少、判定走开后再撑几秒、多久收不到一条广播算设备丢了，都能调

- Scan for nearby BLE devices and pick the one to watch; the picked device can be removed from the same row without touching the master switch

  页面上能扫描附近的蓝牙广播并选一台来盯，选中的设备就在同一行上「移除设备」，不用去关总开关

- Auto unlock is a separate switch and is off by default. Turning it on asks for your login password, which is stored in the keychain on this Mac and typed into the lock screen's password field when you come back — macOS offers no password-free unlock API, so this is the only way it can work

  自动解锁是单独一颗开关，默认关。打开时要填一次登录密码，密码存在本机的钥匙串里，回来时由程序打进锁屏密码框 —— macOS 没有免密解锁接口，这是唯一可行的路子

**注意 / Notes**

- The first scan opens the system Bluetooth prompt; auto unlock additionally needs Accessibility permission (Input Monitoring is not enough). With the lock switch on but no device picked, nothing is armed — there is no target to judge

  第一次点扫描会弹系统蓝牙授权；自动解锁还要「辅助功能」授权（只给「输入监控」不够）。总开关开着但没选设备时不算武装 —— 没有目标就无从判定在不在旁边

- Auto unlock never types blind. Before every chunk of characters it re-checks that the screen is still locked and that the login window still owns secure input; if either changed it aborts the round and leaves the rest of the password untyped. It gives up after three rounds rather than retrying forever

  自动解锁不会盲打。每打一块字符之前都重新确认屏幕还锁着、密码框还在登录窗手里，一旦翻脸这一轮立刻中止，剩下的字符一个都不发；三轮打不开就停手，不会无限重试

- If the display has gone to sleep by the time you get back, the unlock is not thrown away — it waits and types once the screen is awake again. Unlocking the screen yourself in the meantime cancels that pending attempt

  回来的时候如果屏幕已经熄了，这一次解锁不会作废，等屏幕亮起来再补打。这期间你自己把锁解了，挂着的补注入就取消，不会凭空再打一遍密码

- Tested on macOS 27 with a Bluetooth LE watch. Devices that do not advertise (older bands, most earbuds) will not show up in the scan, and a device left behind in the other room keeps the Mac unlocked

  在 macOS 27 上配一块蓝牙手表实测过。不广播的设备（老手环、大部分耳机）扫不到；另一间屋里落下的设备会让这台 Mac 一直不锁
