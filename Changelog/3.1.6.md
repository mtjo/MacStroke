## MacStroke 3.1.6

**新增 / Added**

- The screenshot overlay carries a mode bar along its top edge with four modes: Screenshot, Scrolling screenshot, Screen recording and Extract text

  截图遮罩的顶边上多了一条模式条，四档：截图、滚动截图、录屏、提取文字

- Scrolling screenshot stitches a long image out of a region that scrolls: a wheel event is injected at the centre of the selection, each pair of frames is matched row by row, and only the newly revealed rows are appended. A selection that is already parked at the bottom (a chat log, a page you have read to the end) is stitched upward instead of being reported as a failure. It stops at the end of the content, at the "how many screens" cap, or when the frames stop matching — and whatever has already been stitched is always delivered

  滚动截图把一块能滚的内容接成长图：往选区中心注入滚轮翻页，两帧之间按行比对，只把新露出来的那一段接上去。框住的这块本来就停在底部（聊天记录、已经翻到底的网页）时会翻成向上接，而不是直接报失败。滚到头、撞到「最多接几屏」、或者画面认不上时收场，已经接好的部分照常出图

- Screen recording captures the selected region into an MP4 (no audio). The capsule shows the elapsed time while it runs; stopping writes the file into the screenshot save directory and reports the duration and the path

  录屏把选区采集成 MP4（不带声音），跑的时候胶囊上走已录时长，停止后落盘到截图保存目录并提示时长与路径

- The screenshot preferences page is now "Screenshot / Recording" and carries five new settings: frame rate, maximum duration, whether the pointer is recorded, how far the wheel scrolls per step, and how many screens a long shot may stitch

  偏好设置里「截图」改名「截图/录屏」，新增五项：帧率、时长上限、指针是否进画面、每次滚动的量、长截图最多接几屏

- Every shortcut field that already holds a combination has a clear button on its right (issue #72)

  每个已经录入组合键的快捷键框右侧多了一枚清除按钮，点一下就清空（issue #72）

**修复 / Fixed**

- The annotation toolbar follows the system appearance again: its palette was pinned to one dark set, so on a light desktop the bar and its icons stayed dark while everything else switched

  注解条重新跟随系统明暗：那套配色之前写死成深色，浅色桌面下条子和图标一直是深的，别处都翻了档

**注意 / Notes**

- Scrolling screenshot and screen recording both need the Screen Recording permission. While either runs, the overlay hands the screen back to the app underneath and leaves only an outline and a control capsule — neither of those two is captured, so they never show up in the result

  滚动截图和录屏都要「屏幕录制」授权。这两档跑起来时遮罩把屏幕还给底下的 App，只留一圈描边和一枚控制胶囊，这两扇浮层都不进采集，不会出现在成果里

- Long shots stop stitching on sticky headers and footers, on content that keeps moving (video, marquees) and on lists that jump more than a screen at a time; the part already stitched is still delivered. The closed-source tools this follows behave the same way on those surfaces

  遇到吸顶/吸底工具栏、一直在动的内容（视频、跑马灯）、一次跳超过一屏的列表时，长截图会停止拼接，把已经接好的部分交出来。同类工具在这几种画面上是一样的表现
